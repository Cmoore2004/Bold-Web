Our Purpose & Roots

Built By Black Student Leaders and Professionals for the Next Generation

“BOLD was created by Black undergraduate leaders and professionals who recognized that authentic peer mentorship, early campus exposure, and community support transform how students envision their futures. Rather than waiting for systemic gaps to close, our student leaders took initiative to build the digital and physical spaces they wished had existed when they were in high school.”

Dedicated to youth equity, institutional representation, and self-determined academic achievement.

BOLD (Black Outreach Leadership Directive) is a student-led community initiative committed to increasing and enabling Black student success at the post-secondary level.
BOLD supports Black students exploring or navigating post-secondary education, and works with the families, educators, and organizations around them.
Complete the interest form on our Become a Member page or scan our Student / Executive QR codes. Current student and executive roles are actively recruiting across Ontario.
Send us a request through the Partnerships page or the Contact form and choose “School outreach request”. You can also reach our Outreach team directly at bold.cos@gmail.com.
Connecting students with mentors is a core part of BOLD's work. Current mentorship availability and cohort details are posted on the Programs & Resources page.
Use the partnership inquiry form on the Partnerships page and tell us what you'd like to explore together, or contact bold.cos@gmail.com.
Visit the Donate page to register your interest as a donor or sponsor. Contributions directly support student workshops, campus visit transport, and educational resources.
BOLD is active across Ontario, including high school outreach in London and Southwestern Ontario, the Greater Toronto Area, and expanding regional communities.
Subscribe to our newsletter in the footer, check the Events page, or follow @BOLD.Ontario on Instagram.

Privacy & Trust

Privacy Policy

BOLD is committed to protecting student and family privacy. Information collected via sign-up forms is used exclusively for program communication, mentorship coordination, and event notifications.

We do not sell, rent, or share personal information with third parties. For questions or data requests, contact bold.cos@gmail.com.

Community Guidelines

Terms of Use

BOLD is a student-led community initiative. All resources, workshops, and mentorship pairings are provided in good faith to support post-secondary educational attainment.

All participants, mentors, and members are expected to foster welcoming, respectful, and safe environments for all attendees.
