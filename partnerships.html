Institutional Collaboration

Partner with BOLD

We collaborate with secondary schools, school boards, universities, colleges, Black-led organizations, and community foundations to expand post-secondary access for Black youth.

Collegiate Network & Community Coalition

Collaboration Formats

How We Can Work Together

School Outreach Presentations

Invite BOLD student leaders into your classrooms or assemblies to speak on university pathways, applications, and student resilience.

Campus Immersion Tours

Faculties and departments collaborate with BOLD to host guided walking tours, department spotlights, and lab demonstrations.

Student Group Alliances

We work alongside campus associations to coordinate regional events, co-host panels, and link high school students to upper-year mentors.

Youth Workshops & Panels

Co-host focused seminars on STEM pathways, health science applications, essay writing, and scholarship preparation.

Corporate & Foundation Sponsorship

Provide direct financial or in-kind support for student campus visit transit, workshop supplies, and provincial outreach.

Regional Expansion & Chapters

Support the establishment of new collegiate and regional BOLD branches in London, Windsor, Hamilton, Ottawa, and beyond.
