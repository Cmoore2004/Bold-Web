Educational Outreach

High School Presentations & Pathways
Flagship

Interactive secondary school presentations and pathway seminars designed to help Black students explore university degrees, college diplomas, and skilled trades.

Interactive classroom sessions led by relatable Black university students
Demystifying admissions cutoffs, prerequisite courses, and application deadlines
Financial aid guidance, OSAP tips, and external bursary navigation
Request Presentation for Your School

Mentorship & Guidance

1-on-1 & Cohort Peer Connections
Community

Meaningful pairings connecting secondary students and early-year undergraduates with experienced collegiate mentors across STEM, Business, Humanities, and Health Sciences.

Matching tailored to academic focus, personal goals, and regional institution
Regular check-ins addressing study habits, time management, and imposter syndrome
Guest speakers and panel sessions with Black professionals and alumni
Join as Member or Mentor

Campus Exposure & Immersions

Tours & Experiential Learning
Experiential

Demystifying higher education environments by bringing students onto university and college campuses to experience lecture halls, student lounges, and research facilities firsthand.

Guided walking tours of campus libraries, student centres, and residences
Faculty presentations, lab visits, and simulated university seminar sessions
Transit and meal assistance to ensure full accessibility for participating youth
