Our Real-World Impact in Ontario

At BOLD, impact isn't theoretical. It happens in secondary school classrooms, university lecture halls, and small-group mentorship circles where Black youth envision and pursue their post-secondary ambitions.

400+ Students Reached

Engaged through high school visits, campus tours, and university pathway workshops.

20+ Outreach Events Delivered

Secondary classroom presentations, transition panels, and interactive student assemblies.

40+ BOLD Executives

Dedicated undergraduate leaders and emerging professionals driving initiatives province-wide.

10+ Community & School Partners

Secondary schools, collegiate associations, and community equity organizations collaborating with BOLD.

Frontline Outreach

Mother Teresa Catholic Secondary School Visit

A flagship example of BOLD in action: bringing collegiate leaders and medical students into high schools to demystify university life.

School Outreach Session Ontario Secondary Schools

Direct In-Person Guidance: “The Truth About University”

BOLD leaders visited Mother Teresa Catholic Secondary School to present authentic, relatable perspectives on applying to university and college. Our team answered students’ candid questions about course selections, prerequisite courses, scholarship opportunities, OSAP funding, and adjusting to post-secondary student culture.
