Transparent Stewardship

Where Every Dollar Goes

As an ambitious student-led initiative, we operate with lean overhead to maximize direct educational support for secondary and post-secondary Black youth. Subject to BOLD's verified financial arrangements, contributions help fund:

Educational Outreach: Transportation, materials, and presentation toolkits for high school visits across Ontario.
Campus Exposure Days: Transit subsidies and lunch stipends for participating high school students.
Mentorship Circles: Resource guides, goal-setting workbooks, and honoraria for guest speakers.
Student Resource Hub: Production and free distribution of post-secondary guides and financial toolkits.

Corporate & Community Sponsorship

Businesses, educational foundations, and community organizations can sponsor an entire school outreach tour or host a specialized student panel.
